Neural Storm 2026 · Featured

Sylvan Watch

Compares real Sentinel 2 imagery across years, finds where canopy disappeared, and measures the loss in hectares. Cloud masking, seasonal composites, 27 spectral and temporal features, then georeferenced polygons.

Three detectors were scored on the same held out sites: a spectral rule, a Random Forest and a PyTorch Siamese U-Net. The Random Forest won on validation F1. The U-Net’s training was interrupted, so it is reported as a baseline, not a fair contest.

How I work

Honest by default.

  • Held out means held out. Test sites are kept geographically apart from training sites.
  • Weak results get reported. When a model is not a fair comparison, the write up says so.
  • Claims stop where the data stops. Loss in an image is not proof of illegality.
